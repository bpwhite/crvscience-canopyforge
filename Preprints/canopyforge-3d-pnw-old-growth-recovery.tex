\begin{abstract}
CanopyForge (v1.0.0) is a hardware-accelerated 3D isometric Pacific Northwest (PNW) old-growth watershed recovery and ecological stewardship simulator written in memory-safe Rust across core library modules under GPL-3.0-or-later licensing. Across a 16 x 16 spatial watershed lattice (256 sectors spanning five ecotypes: Remnant Old-Growth Core, Dense Second-Growth Plantation, Incised Salmonid Creek Corridor, Degraded Clearcut Edge, and Upland Oak-Conifer Meadow Ridge), autonomous restoration crews and canopy LiDAR drones execute budget-constrained treatments---variable-density crosscut thinning, prescribed underburns, invasive suppression, Beaver Dam Analogue (BDA) installation, native mycorrhizal underplanting, and snag cavity creation---while strict site-compatibility invariants guarantee mismatched interventions never degrade ecosystem state. The custom three-pass WGPU renderer renders sculpted continuous terrain over columnar basalt cliffs, 2048 x 2048 directional shadow maps, 4x MSAA HDR lighting, animated dual-wave creek water with woven-willow BDAs, and six articulated PNW fauna guilds (Northern Spotted Owl, Oregon Coast Coho Salmon, North American Beaver, Roosevelt Elk, Marbled Murrelet, and Northern Flying Squirrel). Across 8/8 passing tests and deterministic 15-year CLI sweeps (seed 2026), the watershed Ecological Recovery Index (ERI) rises monotonically from 40.42/100 (Year 1, annual grant USD 278,600) to 55.07 (Year 5), 66.92 (Year 8, replicated at 67.10 on seed 1994), and 77.17/100 by Year 15 (annual grant USD 484,800), with Oregon Coast Coho Salmon recovering to 413.0 / 420.0 K (98.3\%).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Silvicultural Recovery Mechanics}
Restoring degraded Pacific Northwest watersheds requires balancing uneven-aged structural complexity, wildfire fuel continuity under rising summer vapor pressure deficit (VPD), incised riparian creek stabilization, and marine-derived nutrient feedbacks between spawning Coho salmon and riparian forests. CanopyForge v1.0.0 unifies those interacting ecological feedback loops inside an offline native Rust simulator backed by a three-pass WGPU 3D renderer and deterministic CLI audit harness.

\section{Three-Pass WGPU Rendering and Zero-Harm Stewardship Rules}
The graphics pipeline in src/wgpu\_renderer.rs executes a 2048 x 2048 Depth32Float sun shadow pass, a 4x MSAA Rgba16Float HDR physically based PBR pass with procedural 3D noise bark mottling and animated dual-wave creek Fresnel reflections, and an ACES filmic tonemap pass across both live interactive viewports and 1600 x 1000 offscreen PNG captures. Ecological interventions enforce site compatibility via evaluate\_and\_apply\_action in src/strategies.rs so out-of-place prescriptions resolve cleanly to No Effect without charging funds or harming stands.

\section{Multi-Year Validation Sweeps and Wildlife Demography}
All 8 unit and integration tests pass. Across deterministic 15-year CLI sweeps (seed 2026), Ecological Recovery Index advances steadily from 40.42 (Year 1) through 55.07 (Year 5), 66.92 (Year 8, reproduced at 67.10 with seed 1994), to 77.17/100 at Year 15. Mean old-growth structural maturity reaches 76.9\%, riparian BDA/LWD coverage reaches 100\%, mean burn risk remains buffered at 4.20\% even during a SevereVpdDrought cycle, and annual stewardship grants scale from USD 278,600 to USD 484,800.
